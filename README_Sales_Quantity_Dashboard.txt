
# Sales & Quantity Metrics Dashboard

## Overview
This Tableau dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales and quantity performance over time and across geographies. It lets users quickly grasp key performance indicators, monthly trends, and top-performing regions.

## Key Features
- **KPIs at a Glance**:
  - Total Sales: $554K
  - Total Quantity: 257K units
  - Total Orders: 1,009

- **Visualizations**:
  - **Monthly Sales Trend**: A line chart showing average monthly sales with annotations for highest and lowest months.
  - **Sales vs Quantity Monthly Comparison**: A bar chart comparing sales and quantity for each month.
  - **Top Countries by Sales**: A filled map highlighting countries based on total sales, with a parameter control to select the top N countries.

## Insights
1. **Sales peaked in January** with the highest monthly total of 85,037 units, while **April saw the lowest** at 31,816 units.
2. The **United Kingdom led in sales** among countries, significantly outperforming others on the map.
3. **November had the highest quantity sold**, indicating possible promotional activity or seasonal demand.


## Filters & Controls
- **Year Filter**: Allows selection of a specific year (currently set to 2011).
- **Top N Country Selector**: Adjusts the number of top countries displayed on the map.

## Usage
This dashboard can support strategic decision-making in:
- Identifying seasonal trends
- Evaluating geographic performance
- Aligning inventory with demand
- Planning promotional campaigns
