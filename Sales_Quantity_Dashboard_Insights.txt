Sales & Quantity Metrics Dashboard - Key Insights

1. Sales Show Early-Year Peak & Mid-Year Recovery
   - January saw the highest sales (~85K), followed by a sharp decline until April (~32K).
   - Sales recovered in July, but didn’t reach early-year levels, indicating seasonal or promotional factors early in the year.

2. Sales and Quantity Don’t Always Correlate
   - In the bar chart, some months (e.g., November) have high quantity but lower sales, hinting at lower-priced items or discounts.
   - This suggests potential revenue leakage despite strong unit movement.

3. United Kingdom Is the Top Market by Sales
   - Based on the map, the UK dominates sales, far ahead of other countries.
   - Suggests it’s a core market and should be a priority for marketing and inventory allocation.
